# Shafal Program: Digital Financial Services (DFS) Achievements

## Overview
The Shafal Program's DFS component focuses on extending formal financial products to migrant workers and their families. The program was structured across two major milestones.

## Milestone I: Preparation & Inception
This foundational phase successfully established the groundwork for the DFS interventions:
*   **Workplan Preparation:** Strategic mapping and scheduling of DFS rollout activities.
*   **Product Design:** Development of tailored financial product solutions suited for migrants and their families.
*   **Branding Materials:** Creation of cohesive brand identity for DFS outreach.
*   **Literacy and Content Materials:** Design of onboarding and promotional content for the financial products.
