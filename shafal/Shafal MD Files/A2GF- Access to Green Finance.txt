Strategic Partnership with United Nations Capital Development Fund (UNCDF)

 

1. Ongoing Project: Shafal (Digital Financial Literacy & Digital Financial Product)

Summary: A UNCDF-backed initiative training for migrant families in digital financial literacy and banking products.
Business Impact: Drives financial inclusion, secures remittance channels, and directly increases the adoption of City Bank’s remittance loan and other core financial products.
Stage: Implementation (Milestones I & II Completed), Milestone III has started
Value & Type: BDT 2.35 Crore (Both DFL & DFS)
Timeline: Oct 2025 – Mar 2027
Reach: 15,000 participants across 6 districts (Chattogram, Feni, Cumilla, Narsingdi, Tangail, Munshiganj)
2. Upcoming Project: Access to Green Climate Finance (A2GF)
·       Stage: Due Diligence completed. Currently Risk team reviewing this transaction facility

·       Project Brief: In partnership with UNCDF, City Bank will deploy a ~BDT 16.5 Crore concessional fund over 36 months to provide accessible green finance to small business at 10% rates. This strategic initiative expands our sustainable portfolio aligned with the Bangladesh Bank Green Taxonomy.

·       Type: Concessional Lending

·       Funding structure: UNCDF provides $449K; City Bank provides $898K (1:2 match fund)

·       Total Fund Size: ~BDT 16.5 Crore / $1.35M

·       Timeline: 24-Month project duration (10-Month disbursement window)

·       Borrowing Rates: 1.00% (Concessional Rate)

·       Disbursement Rate: 10% (Concessional Rate) (there is a soft commitment we will try to give 5-10% loan @9%)

·       Target Customer: Green Finance (Aligned with Bangladesh Bank Green Taxonomy)

·       Agreement Vetting: City Banks legal and other essential teams have reviewed the agreement copy and send back to UNCDF for the adjustments in alignment with banks policy and governance.
