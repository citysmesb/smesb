# Shafal Program: Digital Financial Literacy (DFL) Achievements

## Overview
The Shafal Program's DFL component focuses on improving financial literacy and digital inclusion among targeted communities. The program was structured across two major milestones.

## Milestone I: Preparation & Inception
This foundational phase successfully established the groundwork for the DFL interventions:
*   **Workplan Preparation:** Strategic mapping and scheduling of DFL activities.
*   **Product Design:** Development of targeted digital financial literacy solutions and platforms.
*   **Branding Materials:** Creation of cohesive brand identity for DFL outreach.
*   **Literacy and Content Materials:** Design of contextualized training modules and educational content.
